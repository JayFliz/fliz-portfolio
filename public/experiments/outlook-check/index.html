Is it on Outlook?

Enter an email address to see whether its mail is handled by Microsoft 365 / Outlook — and so might be affected by a Microsoft outage.

How it works: your browser looks up the domain's public DNS records (MX, plus SPF/TXT) via Cloudflare's DNS-over-HTTPS. Nothing is sent to the mailbox and the address itself never leaves your browser — only the domain is looked up.

During an outage, mail to Microsoft-hosted addresses usually isn't lost: sending servers queue and retry for up to a few days. Expect delays rather than bounces.
