About this course

This online support replicates the Warrior Foundation course so the content and processes taught can be embedded and practised until they become second nature. There's no set sequence — dip in and out, and revisit modules as often as you like. Access runs for 12 months and can be extended on request. It's intended only for those who have attended the Warrior Foundation course.

Questions about any of the content? The Warrior Programme team invite you to get in touch for further clarity.
