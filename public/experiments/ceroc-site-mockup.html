Your first night, sorted

Every class night follows the same friendly format — beginners are the heart of it, not an afterthought.

Beginner lesson, 45 minutes

A taxi-rank rotation means you dance with everyone — no partner required, no experience assumed. Three moves per week, taught from scratch.

Freestyle practice

Put the moves together to real music with dance helpers on the floor. This is where it clicks — usually somewhere around week three.
