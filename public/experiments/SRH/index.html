Good to know

Frequently asked questions

In what order do you renovate a house?

Planning and design first, then structural work, plumbing and electrics while the walls are open. Floors, walls and finishes come after, with fixtures and final touches last. Working in this order avoids re-doing work and keeps the project moving.

Can you work room by room while we live in the house?

Usually, yes. We seal off the rooms we're working in, protect everything else, and agree the order of work around how you use the house. Kitchens and bathrooms are sequenced so you're never without both at once.

How do you price a job?

We visit, measure up and talk through what you want, then give you a written quote broken down by room and stage. If something unexpected turns up once we open things up, we price the change before doing the work — never after.

Why do you photograph everything?

Because the difference between a good renovation and a bad one is hidden behind the finished surfaces. Photographing every stage keeps us honest, gives you a record of exactly what's in your walls and floors, and shows future clients how we work.
